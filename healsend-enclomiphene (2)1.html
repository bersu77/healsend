How enclomiphene works

Your body already makes testosterone.
Enclomiphene tells it to make more.

Unlike TRT, which replaces what your body makes, enclomiphene gently restarts your own production by targeting one tiny signal in your brain.

Why this matters: Traditional TRT shuts down this entire pathway because your body senses external testosterone and stops making its own. Enclomiphene does the opposite — it keeps the whole system running, just at a higher set point. That's why fertility and testicular size are preserved.

How quickly will I notice results? +

Most patients feel changes within 2 weeks — better morning energy, improved mood, returning libido. Bloodwork at week 6 typically shows total T doubled or close to it. Full benefits stabilize around 12 weeks as your HPG axis fully recalibrates.

Is enclomiphene really as effective as TRT? +

It depends on your starting point. For most men with secondary hypogonadism (the majority), enclomiphene reaches the same eugonadal T range as TRT — without the dependency and fertility tradeoffs. For severe primary hypogonadism (where the testicles can't respond), TRT may still be needed. Your clinician will help you understand which category you fall into based on your labs.

What are the side effects? +

Side effects are typically mild and infrequent. The most reported are headache, mild nausea, hot flashes, and occasional mood changes. Unlike TRT, enclomiphene does not commonly cause acne, hair loss, polycythemia (high red blood cell count), or testicular shrinkage. If you experience anything, message your clinician — dose adjustments resolve most issues.

Will it really preserve my fertility? +

Yes. Multiple peer-reviewed studies (Wiehle 2014, Kaminetsky 2013) show enclomiphene maintains sperm counts and testicular volume because it works upstream of your testicles — increasing the LH/FSH signal rather than replacing testosterone. TRT, by contrast, suppresses both signals and can cause infertility within months.

Can I stop anytime? What happens? +

Yes. Because enclomiphene works with your body's natural production rather than replacing it, stopping doesn't trigger the rebound or shutdown that TRT can cause. Your levels return to baseline over a few weeks. Your clinician will guide you if you want to discontinue.

Do I need lab work first? +

Yes — and we include it. Before any prescription, you'll get a full hormone panel (Total T, Free T, LH, FSH, SHBG, Estradiol, PSA, Hematocrit) drawn at your home. Recent labs from the past 6 months can also be uploaded if they include the right markers.

Which states do you serve? +

HealSend is licensed in 47 states. Eligibility is verified during the assessment before any charge. If we can't serve your state, you don't pay anything and we'll point you toward alternatives.

Is enclomiphene FDA-approved? +

Enclomiphene is a stereoisomer of clomiphene citrate, which is FDA-approved. Enclomiphene itself is currently prescribed off-label by physicians for male hypogonadism — a very common practice (40% of all U.S. prescriptions are off-label). Your clinician will discuss this and any alternatives during your consultation.

HealSend operates as a cash-pay telehealth service. Every plan is FSA and HSA eligible, and we provide itemized receipts for any reimbursement claims. Most patients find this faster than navigating insurance prior-auth for hormone therapy.
