01 / The break

Telehealth pharmacy is broken by design. We rebuilt the supply chain around the prescriber.

i.

Opaque markups

Most telehealth pharmacies buy at compounded wholesale, list at multiples of cost, and call it convenience. You and your patients overpay because nobody publishes the raw acquisition cost. We change that.

ii.

Single-source risk

One pharmacy goes on backorder, your weight-loss program halts. We route across 14 partner pharmacies in 38 states with active redundancy.

iii.

Compliance theatre

Sketchy vendors, no MOAs, no chain-of-custody. We vet every facility quarterly and surface audit-ready documentation in your portal.

02 / Process

A GPO that behaves like infrastructure, not a sales funnel.

i
STEP 01

Apply in 4 minutes

Verify your DEA, NPI, and state license. We auto-validate against NPPES and state boards — no PDF uploads, no notarization games.

ii
STEP 02

Contracted in 5 days

Average time from submitted application to executed MSA is five business days. One master agreement covers every pharmacy in the network.

iii
STEP 03

Order via portal or API

Start prescribing the same day your contract closes. Use the Vantascript portal or drop our REST + FHIR API into your existing EHR — Elation, Akute, Healthie, or custom.

iv
STEP 04

One invoice, monthly

Net-30 consolidated billing across all pharmacies. Real-time spend dashboard. Audit logs exportable in CSV, JSON, and FHIR.
