What's actually in SURGE? +

SURGE is a clinician-prescribed troche (dissolvable lozenge) that combines two PDE5 inhibitors: Sildenafil (the active ingredient in Viagra, fast onset) and Tadalafil (the active ingredient in Cialis, long window) in a single dose.

Dosing is calibrated to your case by your clinician — typically 25–50mg Sildenafil + 5–10mg Tadalafil per troche. Compounded by US-licensed 503A pharmacies and shipped within 48 hours.

How is this different from getting a prescription from my doctor? +

Mechanically: it's the same prescription, from a US-licensed physician, dispensed by a US-licensed pharmacy. Functionally: you skip the awkward in-person visit, the urology referral, and the 2–3 week timeline.

SURGE is also a HealSend-specific compounded formulation — your local pharmacy won't have it as a stock item. The dose and form (dissolvable troche) is something most clinics don't offer.

Is the generic version really the same as branded Viagra / Cialis? +

Yes. The active ingredients are identical. Branded Viagra (sildenafil) and branded Cialis (tadalafil) lost patent protection years ago, which means generic versions with the same active ingredient at the same dose are FDA-equivalent.

The difference is price — generic versions are typically 70–90% less expensive than branded equivalents while delivering the same clinical effect.

How quickly will I get my first dose? +

Typical timeline: 90-second intake → clinician review within 24 hours → medication ships within 48 hours → arrives in 2 days. Most members are 48–72 hours from intake to first dose.

If you indicate urgency in the intake, expedited review is available at no extra cost.

What are the side effects? +

PDE5 inhibitors are among the most-studied medications in modern medicine. Most common side effects: headache, facial flushing, nasal congestion, mild dyspepsia. These typically resolve within hours and are dose-dependent — lower doses produce fewer side effects.

Serious adverse events are rare. Critical safety note: these medications interact with nitrate medications (used for heart conditions) and certain blood pressure medications. Your clinician screens for these during intake and won't prescribe if your case isn't safe.

What if the first dose doesn't work? +

Dose finding is a normal part of ED treatment. If the first dose isn't effective, your clinician will adjust at no extra charge, typically within 24 hours. Most members find their effective dose within the first 2 attempts.

Our 60-day effective-dose guarantee covers this: if your clinician can't find an effective dose within 60 days and 3 attempts, we refund your most recent month.

What if I have heart disease, diabetes, or take other medications? +

Your intake asks about every relevant medical condition and medication. Your clinician screens for interactions before prescribing. Nitrate medications (for chest pain or heart conditions) are an absolute contraindication — these will not be co-prescribed.

Diabetes, controlled hypertension, and most cardiovascular conditions are generally compatible with PDE5 inhibitor therapy, but the clinician makes the final call based on your specific case.

Is the shipping really discreet? +

Yes. Packaging is plain and unmarked. Shipping labels reference HealSend (not the medication). No medication name visible from the outside of the package. Direct from US-licensed compounding pharmacy.

Can I switch protocols later? +

Yes, anytime. Many members start with Sildenafil, switch to SURGE for the longer window, or move to daily Tadalafil as their needs evolve. Switching is a clinician-approved change with no friction — message your care team.

Is this legal? Is it FDA-approved? +

Sildenafil and Tadalafil are FDA-approved medications with decades of safety data. They're among the most-prescribed pharmaceuticals globally.

SURGE is a compounded formulation — the active ingredients are the same FDA-approved compounds, combined in a custom troche format by US-licensed 503A pharmacies under prescription. Compounded medications are legally prescribed every day in the US.
