Your questions.
Honest answers.

Everything you want to know before getting started. When you start your plan, you'll have unlimited access to clinicians for virtual consults or support.

Which states do you serve for TRT? +

HealSend is licensed in 47 states. Your eligibility is confirmed during the assessment before any charge. If we can't ship to your state, you don't pay — and we'll let you know what to do next.

Do you accept insurance? +

HealSend operates as a cash-pay telehealth service. We don't bill insurance, but every plan is FSA and HSA eligible — most members use pre-tax dollars. We provide itemized receipts for any reimbursement attempts.

What medications do HealSend clinicians prescribe? +

Our primary protocol uses Testosterone Cypionate (compounded with MCT oil) administered as weekly subcutaneous injections. Optional add-ons include hCG for fertility preservation, Anastrozole for estrogen control, and Tadalafil for daily ED + prostate support.

Does the price stay the same as my dose changes? +

Yes. Your monthly price is locked in regardless of dose adjustments. Your clinician titrates your protocol based on bloodwork — at no extra cost to you.

Does my plan include lab work and supplies? +

Every plan includes your medication, syringes, alcohol pads, sharps disposal, your initial hormone panel, and quarterly follow-up labs. The annual plan adds free hCG and four panels per year.

Will TRT affect my fertility? +

Yes — like all forms of TRT, injectable testosterone can suppress sperm production. If preserving fertility is a priority, our TRT + hCG plan adds hCG to maintain testicular function. Discuss this with your clinician during your intake.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. Cancel anytime in your dashboard — no calls, no retention scripts. Annual plan members receive a prorated refund for unused months. Your clinician will help you taper safely if you want to come off.
